Island transfers

Speedboat transfers between the islands

One fast boat a day links Koh Chang, Koh Wai, Koh Mak and Koh Kood – south in the morning, north around midday. Fares are one way and per person; the return leg can be on any date.

How it works

Book

Send us the date, the route and your hotel. We confirm by WhatsApp or email and tell you how to pay.

Pick-up

On Koh Chang our taxi collects you from your hotel before the 09:00 departure, free of charge. On the other islands you board at the pier.

On board

Luggage travels with you. Life jackets are on board; keep electronics in a dry bag – it is a speedboat.

Drop-off

We land at the pier closest to your resort. On Koh Kood that means the resort piers along the west coast – tell us where you are staying.

Where to board

  • Koh ChangKai Bae Beach – our own pier. Free pick-up from your hotel on Koh Chang.
  • Koh WaiMost resort piers on the west coast. Tell us your resort when you book.
  • Koh MakMakathanee Resort pier (Ao Kao) or Koh Rayang.
  • Koh KoodMost resort piers on the west coast. Tell us your resort when you book.

Children

Children 4–6 years pay half. Infants up to 3 years travel free on a parent’s lap (no stroller). Strollers 200 THB.

Season

The scheduled boat runs in high season, roughly from the start of November to the end of April. In the green season the sea on this coast can be rough and we run private transfers only when conditions allow – ask us.

Questions & answers

Can I bring luggage on the transfer?

Yes. Normal suitcases and backpacks travel with you on the boat at no extra charge. Keep phones and cameras in a dry bag – the boats are fast and spray is normal.

Where exactly do you drop me on Koh Kood?

At your resort’s pier if it has one, otherwise the nearest pier on the west coast (Klong Chao, Bang Bao Bay and the beaches in between). Resorts south of Bang Bao Bay are not on the regular route – tell us your resort when you book and we will say exactly where you land and how to get on from there.

Do I have to book the return at the same time?

No. Fares are one way and each leg is booked separately, so you can come back on any date the boat runs. A return is simply two one-way fares.

What if the sea is rough?

The captain decides on the morning. If it is unsafe we postpone or cancel, and you choose another date or a full refund. On this coast that mostly happens in the green season (May–October), which is why the scheduled boat runs in high season only.

How do I pay?

Cash in Thai baht at the pier, or bank transfer in advance. Private charters are confirmed with a deposit. There are no booking fees when you book direct with us.
