Island guide

The islands

Sixteen islands within reach of Kai Bae. The four on the daily transfer route you can stay on; the rest we visit on snorkelling trips and charters.

Koh ChangKoh WaiKoh MakKoh KoodKoh RangKoh YakKoh NokKoh MapringKoh LaoyaKoh KlumKoh KradadKoh KhamKoh YuakKoh ManKoh PleeKoh Rom
Daily transfer Koh ChangKoh Chang is where every trip starts and ends. Our pier is on Kai Bae Beach, in the middle of the west coast, and our pick-up trucks collect guests from hotels along the whole coast – White Sand, Klong Prao, Kai Bae, Lonely Beach and Bang Bao – before each departure. Visited by: Daily transfer
Daily transfer Koh WaiKoh Wai is the first stop on the way south. It has a few simple resorts, no roads and a reef that starts a few metres from the sand, which makes it a favourite with families and first-time snorkellers. Transfers drop and collect at the resort piers. Visited by: Koh Rang 5 islands (full day) · Koh Wai, Laoya & Klum (half day) · Daily transfer
Daily transfer Koh MakKoh Mak lies about an hour south of Koh Chang. It is flat enough to explore by bicycle and much quieter than Koh Chang, with long sandy beaches at Ao Kao and Ao Suan Yai. Our transfers use the Makathanee Resort pier on Ao Kao; Koh Rayang, a tiny private island just off Koh Mak, is served on request. Visited by: Daily transfer
Daily transfer Koh KoodKoh Kood (Ko Kut) is two hours by speedboat from Koh Chang and is worth staying on rather than day-tripping. The west coast has a string of beautiful beaches – Klong Chao, Bang Bao, Ao Phrao – each with its own resorts. Our boats drop and collect at the resort piers along this coast; please tell us where you are staying so we bring you to the right pier. Visited by: Daily transfer
Marine national parkKoh RangKoh Rang is a protected island with a ranger station, small white-sand bays and the healthiest coral in the archipelago. The water is clear and the fish life is rich. A national park fee applies (200 THB for foreign adults, 100 THB for children, 40 THB for Thai nationals) and is paid at the park, not to us. Visited by: Koh Rang 5 islands (full day)
Marine national parkKoh YakKoh Yak Yai and Koh Yak Lek are small granite islets near Koh Rang. There is no beach, but the coral around them is shallow and full of fish, so you snorkel straight from the boat. Part of the marine national park. Visited by: Koh Rang 5 islands (full day)
Marine national parkKoh NokKoh Nok is a small island in the Koh Rang group. Boats moor off the reef for a snorkel stop. Part of the marine national park.
Marine national parkKoh MapringKoh Mapring sits between Koh Rang and Koh Wai and is usually the least crowded stop of the day. Good coral gardens, anemones and clownfish. Part of the marine national park. Visited by: Koh Rang 5 islands (full day)
Koh LaoyaKoh Laoya is a group of three small islands just south of Koh Chang. The main island has one resort and a beautiful white-sand beach – the swimming and the photos are the reason to stop here rather than the snorkelling. Visited by: Koh Wai, Laoya & Klum (half day)
Koh KlumKoh Klum is the large island you see from Bang Bao. It is almost untouched, with one small beach on the south-east side and shallow, calm water that is easy for children and nervous swimmers. Visited by: Koh Wai, Laoya & Klum (half day)
Koh KradadKoh Kradad is flat, privately owned and home to a herd of deer that roam free – some tame enough to hand-feed. The island charges a small landing fee, which includes a short tractor tour. A long, deserted beach runs along the east side.
Koh KhamKoh Kham is a small private island a few minutes from Koh Mak. Its beach mixes white sand with dark volcanic boulders and the snorkelling off the beach is good. A landing fee is charged by the island.
Koh YuakKoh Yuak is the largest of a cluster of small islands just off Klong Prao and Kai Bae beaches. It is the only one with a proper white-sand beach and has an easy, shallow reef – perfect for a half-day trip when you do not want to spend the whole day on the water. Visited by: Koh Yuak 4 islands (half day)
Koh ManKoh Man is a rocky islet next to Koh Yuak with coral heads, starfish and reef fish in shallow water. Visited by: Koh Yuak 4 islands (half day)
Koh PleeKoh Plee is another islet in the Koh Yuak cluster. The current between the islands brings soft corals and sea fans to the rocks. Visited by: Koh Yuak 4 islands (half day)
Koh RomKoh Rom completes the four-island half-day circuit off Kai Bae. Boulders, reef fish and clear water a few minutes from the pier. Visited by: Koh Yuak 4 islands (half day)

Private charter

Seven itineraries, five boat sizes from 4 to 30 guests. Choose the islands, the order and the pace. Prices are per boat, so for a family or a group a private charter often costs little more than a join-in trip.
