About us

Thirty years between the islands

N. Kai Bae Hut Speedboat began in 1995 with boats taking visitors across to Koh Chang, in the days before the car ferry made the crossing easy. As the island grew, so did we: first snorkelling trips to the reefs off Kai Bae, then the marine park, and finally the daily speedboat link that connects Koh Chang with Koh Wai, Koh Mak and Koh Kood.

Today we are the largest speedboat operator on the island, still run by the same family from our own pier on Kai Bae Beach. Our captains and crew are local; many have worked these waters since the beginning and know every reef and every resort pier by name.

We hold a Tourism Authority of Thailand tour operator licence (no. 13/03131) and are registered at 10/8 Moo 4, Koh Chang, Trat 23170.

What you can expect
