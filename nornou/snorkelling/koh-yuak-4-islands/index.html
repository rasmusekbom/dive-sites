Morning or afternoon

Koh Yuak, Koh Man, Koh Plee & Koh Rom – four islands in three hours

09:00–12:00 / 13:00–16:00Koh Yuak · Koh Man · Koh Plee · Koh RomFruit & water (no lunch)Hotel pick-up included

Three hours, four small islands, three kilometres from the beach. Choose the morning (09:00–12:00) or the afternoon (13:00–16:00).

Koh Yuak and its neighbours lie just off Klong Prao and Kai Bae, so within ten minutes of leaving the pier you are in the water. Koh Yuak has the beach – white sand and a shallow reef that is easy for children – while Koh Man, Koh Plee and Koh Rom are rocky islets where you snorkel from the boat over coral heads, starfish and sea fans.

Fruit and drinking water are served on board (no lunch on this trip). Mask, snorkel, life jacket and a float board are included, and so is the shared taxi to and from your hotel.

The day

  • 08:30 / 12:30Pick-up from your hotel on Koh Chang
  • 09:00 / 13:00Depart Kai Bae pier
  • 09:15–11:30 / 13:15–15:30Koh Yuak beach, then Koh Man, Koh Plee and Koh Rom
  • 12:00 / 16:00Back at Kai Bae; taxi to your hotel

What is included

  • Speedboat with captain and crew
  • Mask, snorkel and life jacket
  • Float board to borrow on the boat
  • Underwater photos by our crew
  • Fruit and drinking water
  • Shared taxi (songthaew) from and to your hotel on Koh Chang

Not included

  • Lunch
  • Towel and sunscreen

The islands

Koh YuakThe closest snorkelling island to Koh Chang – 3 km off Kai Bae, with a white beach and a shallow reef.Koh ManOne of the small islands in the Koh Yuak cluster; snorkelling from the boat.Koh PleeA small islet in the Koh Yuak group with soft corals and sea fans.Koh RomThe last stop of the four-island half day, a rocky islet with reef fish.

Questions & answers

Is the trip suitable for children?

Yes – the half-day trips especially. Children’s life jackets and small masks are on board, and stops like Koh Klum and Koh Yuak have calm, shallow water and a beach. Children 4–10 pay the child fare; under 4 travel free on a parent’s lap.

Do I need to be a good swimmer?

No. Everyone gets a life jacket, and there is a float board on the boat to hold on to. The crew are in the water with you and choose calm, shallow spots for beginners and children.

Where do you pick me up?

From your hotel or resort anywhere on Koh Chang’s west coast, by shared songthaew taxi. We confirm the time the evening before; it is usually 30–45 minutes before the boat leaves.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, a towel, sunscreen (reef-safe if you can), a hat and a dry bag for your phone. Masks, snorkels, life jackets, water and fruit are on board. A little cash for the national park fee on the Koh Rang trip.
