Koh Rang & the marine park – five islands in a day

09:00–16:00Koh Yak · Koh Mapring · Koh Rang · Koh WaiLunch includedHotel pick-up includedNational park fee extra

Our classic full day. Five stops in and around the Mu Ko Chang Marine National Park, where the water is clearest and the coral healthiest.

We collect you from your hotel around 08:15–08:45 and leave Kai Bae at 09:00. The speedboat reaches the first snorkel stop in about 40 minutes – far quicker than the wooden tour boats – so you spend the day in the water, not getting there.

Each stop is roughly an hour: Koh Yak Yai and Koh Yak Lek for shallow coral straight off the boat, Koh Mapring for anemones and table corals, Koh Rang for its white-sand bay and the richest fish life, and Koh Wai for a last swim from the beach. Lunch, fruit and drinking water are served on board.

Snorkel, mask and life jacket are included, and there is a float board to borrow if you would rather paddle than swim. Our crew take underwater photos along the way and share them with you afterwards.

  • 16:00Back at Kai Bae; taxi to your hotel

Questions & answers

What is the national park fee and who collects it?

Koh Rang, Koh Yak, Koh Nok and Koh Mapring are inside the Mu Ko Chang Marine National Park. The park charges 200 THB per foreign adult, 100 THB per child and 40 THB for Thai nationals, paid at the park on the day. It is set by the park, not by us, and is not included in the trip price.

Do I need to be a good swimmer?

No. Everyone gets a life jacket, and there is a float board on the boat to hold on to. The crew are in the water with you and choose calm, shallow spots for beginners and children.

I get seasick. Is a speedboat a good idea?

Speedboats spend far less time at sea than the slow tour boats, and the morning sea is usually calm. Sit at the back, look at the horizon, and take a tablet an hour before if you know you are prone to it.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, a towel, sunscreen (reef-safe if you can), a hat and a dry bag for your phone. Masks, snorkels, life jackets, water and fruit are on board. A little cash for the national park fee on the Koh Rang trip.
