Your own speedboat for the day

Questions & answers

What is included in a private charter?

The boat with captain and crew for the day, two-way taxi between your hotel and our pier, snorkelling equipment for everyone, drinking water and fruit, and a simple Thai lunch on full-day itineraries. National park fees are extra.

Can we change the itinerary?

Yes. The seven itineraries are starting points. Tell us which islands matter most to you and how much time you want in the water versus on a beach, and we will build the day around it and quote a price.

Is a charter good for families?

Very. You set the pace, stop when the children are tired and skip the crowded spots. Children’s life jackets are on board. Per person, a ten-seater shared by two families often costs about the same as a join-in trip.

What happens if the weather is bad?

If the captain cancels for safety you get another date or a full refund. Light rain does not stop a trip – the water is still warm and clear.

How do I pay?

Cash in Thai baht at the pier, or bank transfer in advance. Private charters are confirmed with a deposit. There are no booking fees when you book direct with us.
