About us

A Koh Chang family business since 1995

N. Kai Bae Hut Speedboat started with the ferry to Koh Chang and grew into the largest speedboat operator on the island: our own pier at Kai Bae, a fleet from small four-seaters to thirty-seat twin-engine boats, and a licensed tour operator (TAT licence 13/03131).

About us

The islands

Sixteen islands within reach of Kai Bae – from the coral of the marine park to the beaches of Koh Kood.

The islands
Koh RangThe heart of the Mu Ko Chang Marine National Park and the best snorkelling in the area.Koh WaiA small, quiet island 30 minutes south of Koh Chang with clear water and easy snorkelling straight from the beach.Koh MakFlat, calm and laid-back – an island of coconut and rubber plantations, long beaches and bicycles.Koh KoodThe furthest island in the group: empty white beaches, clear water and a slow pace of life.
