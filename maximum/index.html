About us

The first freediving school in Siargao

Maximum Freediving started in Anilao, Batangas, and opened its Siargao school in General Luna to bring the same thing here: proper Molchanovs and AIDA instruction, small groups, and a coach in the water on every dive. Founded by Nate Lopez and Ara Neyra, with a team of Wave 2 coaches trained in-house.

About us

Where we dive

Ten to thirty minutes by bangka from General Luna: cabbage corals, the Daku drop-off, the training line and, for the advanced, Blue Cathedral.

Dive sites
5–15 mCabbage Coral GardenThe shallow field of cabbage corals off Daku that made Siargao famous – our home site for Intro sessions, fun dives and photoshoots.8–20 mDaku IslandThe biggest of the three islands: a reef slope, the Arch and the Hills, with schools of fusiliers and jacks.10–35 mThe training lineOur buoy and line in 30+ metres of blue water off Daku – where the courses and line-training mornings happen.18–40 mBlue CathedralSiargao’s famous cavern in 18–40 metres – for advanced freedivers on calm days.
