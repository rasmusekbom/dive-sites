About us

The first freediving school in Siargao

Maximum Freediving began in Anilao, Batangas – the cradle of Philippine freediving – as a school and a hostel for freedivers, and grew into a community with locations in Batangas, Manila and Siargao. The Siargao school in General Luna was the first on the island dedicated to freediving rather than a sideline of a surf shop or a scuba centre.

The school was founded by Nate Lopez, a Molchanovs instructor (and an award-winning actor), and Ara Neyra, an MFI mermaid instructor. They still teach and shoot most sessions, and they have trained a team of Wave 2 Molchanovs coaches from the island. Our underwater photographer, JK Guinoo, filmed the underwater scenes of the VIU series “Love Siargao” with us.

We teach the Molchanovs and AIDA systems and mermaid courses through the Pacific Mermaid Academy. What we care about is the same in all of them: a relaxed diver, a safety on every dive, and pictures that show you what you did.

How we work

Never alone

One coach per small group and a safety on every single dive. Buddy rules are the first thing we teach.

Doing less

Freediving is relaxation, not effort. Non-swimmers learn to float before they learn to dive.

Prices you can see

What is on this page is what you pay. Boat, gear and photos are included, not added at the counter.

The camera is always on

Your coach photographs and films every session. You leave with the pictures the same day.
