FAQ

Questions & answers

The questions we get every day, answered honestly. Anything else – ask us on WhatsApp.

Do I need to know how to swim?

No. A lot of beginners think swimming is about effort; freediving is about reducing it. The wetsuit and the water float you, you rest on a buoy between dives, and your coach is within arm’s reach. Non-swimmers do the Intro session with us every week. For the certification courses (Wave 1, AIDA 2 and above) you need to be able to swim 200 m.

How deep will I go?

On an Intro session most people reach 5–10 metres, and depth is not the point – comfort is. Wave 1 and AIDA 2 certify you to 20 m, Wave 2 and AIDA 3 to 30 m. You start to feel the pressure in the first metre or two, which is why equalisation is the first thing we teach.

Is freediving safe?

Yes, as long as you follow the instructions. The rules are simple: never alone, one up one down, a safety on every dive, and no pushing past what feels comfortable. Our coaches are trained in rescue and every session carries a buoy, a line and a first-aid kit.

Do I need my own gear?

No. Mask, snorkel, long fins, wetsuit and weights are included in every session and course. If you continue, you can buy or rent gear later – we rent fins, masks and buoys by the day.

What should I bring?

Swimwear (worn under the wetsuit), a towel, sunscreen that is reef-safe, water and a light breakfast eaten at least an hour before. Bring your certification card if you have one. Leave valuables at your accommodation – the boat is a bangka.

AIDA or Molchanovs – which should I choose?

Both are excellent and the water skills are almost the same. Molchanovs gives you a lifetime online training programme and a strong community; AIDA is the largest organisation and the usual route for competitions and instructor training. If you already hold a card from one, continue with that one. If not, tell us your goals and we will recommend.

How many days does a certification course take?

Wave 1 and AIDA 2 take three mornings; AIDA 1 one; Wave 2 and AIDA 3 three to four. We can spread the days out if you also want to surf – many people dive in the morning and surf in the afternoon.

Is the theory online?

Yes. The Molchanovs and AIDA manuals are online and included. Read them before you arrive and the mornings are all water; if you prefer, we go through the theory together at the HQ.

What are the requirements?

16 years or older (younger with a parent’s consent), a 200 m swim, and a signed medical questionnaire. If you have asthma, ear or sinus problems or a heart condition, talk to your doctor first and tell us.

How long is the certification valid?

For life. Your card is digital and you can show it at any school in the world. If you have not dived for a year or more, a refresher morning on the line is a good idea.

What do I need before Wave 2?

A Wave 1, AIDA 2 or equivalent certification and comfort at 15–20 metres. If your last dive was a while ago, book a Line Training morning first – we will tell you honestly whether you are ready.

Who is line training for?

Certified freedivers, or people with real experience, who want a coached morning: to refresh after a break, to work on equalisation or technique, or to keep depth between trips.

How deep is the line?

The buoy sits in 30+ metres of blue water off Daku; you dive to whatever depth you and your coach agree – typically between 10 and 30 metres.

Who books a private session?

Nervous beginners, children with a parent, couples on a short visit, people with a schedule that does not fit the group sessions – anyone who wants the coach to themselves.

Can two people share a private session?

Yes. The price covers the boat and the coach for one person; a second person usually joins for the cost of gear and certificate. Ask us.

Can I join a fun dive as a beginner?

You need an Intro session or a certification first – we must know you can equalise and duck-dive safely. Many people do the Intro one morning and a fun dive the next.

What if we are fewer than the minimum?

The daily fun dive runs with three people, the tri-island with four. If you are fewer we try to fill the boat with other divers; otherwise you can book a private fun dive.

Do fun dives include photos?

Yes – your coach photographs and films the session and shares the files the same day. For edited images or a reel, book the underwater photoshoot.

Which islands does the tri-island fun dive visit?

Daku, Naked and Guyam – the three islands off General Luna. Two or three sites depending on the sea, with beach time in between.

When is a private fun dive worth it?

When you want your own schedule, two sites of your choice and the camera on your group alone – or when you are fewer than the group minimum.

Do I need experience for a photoshoot?

You should be comfortable freediving to 5–10 metres and holding still without a mask for a few seconds. An Intro session or fun dive first makes a big difference. Beginners can book the Intro + photography combination.

When do I get the pictures?

Raw files the same day; the five edited images (or the colour-graded reel) within a few days, by download link.

What should I wear for a shoot?

Plain colours photograph best underwater – white, red, yellow, black. Long dresses and flowing fabric look wonderful and we have done them; bring what you like and we plan it together.

Can I combine a shoot with a course?

Yes – Intro + photography is a popular half day, and certified freedivers often add a shoot to a fun dive. Ask us for the combined price.

When is the best time to freedive in Siargao?

March to May has the calmest, clearest water; the dry season runs until about October. During the north-east monsoon (November–February) the Pacific swell can be big, and we pick sheltered sites or move a session to the next day. Water is 27–30 °C all year.

How do I pay?

Deposit by GCash or bank transfer to confirm; the balance in cash, GCash or transfer at the HQ on the morning. Ask us if you need a card payment link.

Can I book accommodation with a course?

Yes. Tell us your dates when you book and we will hold the unit or a partner villa for you.

Where is the accommodation?

Our own unit is next to the HQ in Poblacion 5, General Luna, a few minutes from Tourism Road. Partner villas are also in General Luna.

How do I pay for the room?

Same as the sessions – a deposit to confirm, the balance on arrival.

Can children freedive?

From about ten years, with a parent’s consent and ideally with the parent in the water. Families do the Intro session together every week. Certification courses start at 16 (younger case by case).

Any medical restrictions?

Do not freedive with a cold, sinus or ear infection, or within 12 hours after scuba diving. Asthma, heart conditions, ear surgery and pregnancy need a doctor’s clearance. You fill in a medical questionnaire before the session.
