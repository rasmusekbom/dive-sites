Seastar / FAQ

Questions and answers

The things we hear most often. If your answer is not here, call us.

Do I need to be able to swim?

Yes, but not fast. Before Open Water you need to swim 200 metres unaided (or 300 metres with mask, snorkel and fins) and float or tread water for ten minutes. Nothing is timed. For a try-dive it is enough that you are comfortable in water.

Isn’t it freezing?

In a dry suit you stay dry and wear ordinary clothes underneath. What gets cold is your hands and face. We train for these conditions, which is exactly why the dry suit course exists.

How long does the Open Water course take?

You do the theory as e-learning at home at your own pace. Then come the pool sessions and four open water dives. How that spreads across the calendar depends on when the course runs – get in touch and we will tell you what is scheduled.

What does it cost?

Prices vary by course and season. Call +46 72 561 02 40 or email info@seastardiving.se and you will get a current price for the course you are interested in.

Do I need my own equipment?

No. All equipment is included during a course. Many people do want their own mask fairly quickly, since fit is personal. The rest you can rent until you know what you want.

How old do I have to be?

From 10 for try-dives and Open Water (Junior). Advanced from 12, Rescue from 12, Divemaster and instructor from 18.

I dived ten years ago. Do I have to retake the course?

No, the certification is for life. But if it has been a long time we recommend a refresher session with an instructor before you head out. Get in touch and we will arrange it.

Can I take the course in English?

Yes. We teach in Swedish, English, Danish and Norwegian.

Can I dive with asthma, diabetes or on medication?

Often yes, but that is not for us to decide. You fill in a medical statement before the course, and some answers require a certificate from a doctor with dive medicine training. Contact us in good time so it can be sorted.

Do you fill air for non-members?

Yes. We fill air, nitrox and trimix during shop opening hours. Bring valid certification for the gas and a cylinder that is in test.

Do you service equipment not bought from you?

Yes. Drop it off in the shop and we will come back with what needs doing and what it costs before we start.

Do you have your own boat?

Yes, we have a dive boat and run trips in the Stockholm archipelago through the season.

Start with a conversation

Tell us where you are and we will suggest a route. No experience required to pick up the phone.
