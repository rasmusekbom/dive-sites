PADI Open Water Diver

The entry-level sport diving course and the certification most divers hold. Once you pass, you can dive with a buddy to 18 metres, anywhere in the world.

Beginner4 daysTo 18 mPADI Open Water Diver

The course has three parts: theory, confined water (pool) and four open water dives. You do the theory as e-learning at your own pace before we meet – that way we spend our time together in the water instead of in a classroom.

In the pool we build the skills calmly: assembling your kit, controlling buoyancy, clearing your mask, sharing air. Once those are solid we head into open water.

You need to swim 200 metres (or 300 m with mask, snorkel and fins) and float or tread water for ten minutes. Nothing is timed – we only want to see that you are comfortable in water.

What you learn

  • Dive theory and tables
  • Assembling and checking your kit
  • Buoyancy control
  • Mask clearing and air sharing
  • Compass navigation
  • Buddy checks and planning

What's included

  • Instructor
  • PADI e-learning
  • All equipment
  • Pool sessions
  • Open water dives
