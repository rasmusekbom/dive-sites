Technical diving

For when the limits of sport diving start to feel like limits. We are a TecRec centre, we teach on rebreathers and we mix the gas ourselves.

Technical diving means dives where you cannot go straight to the surface – because you have decompression to pay off, or a ceiling above you. It takes more equipment, more planning and considerably more discipline.

We take it in steps. Tec 40 is a gentle crossover from sport diving with limited decompression. Tec 45 and Tec 50 open up real decompression diving with several gases. Trimix takes you past the depth where air stops being a good idea.

Alongside that we teach rebreather diving. A closed circuit means quieter dives, warmer breathing gas and far longer bottom times – and a completely different standard of care before and after the dive.

We blend nitrox and trimix in house and sell CO2 absorbent over the counter. You do not need to plan your dives around somebody else’s delivery schedule.

Courses we run

Tec 40The first step into technical diving. Limited decompression, one deco gas with up to 50 % oxygen. · PADI Tec 40 · To 40 m
Tec 45Real decompression diving to 45 metres, with full deco gas and redundancy. · PADI Tec 45 · To 45 m
Tec 50To 50 metres with several deco gases and accelerated decompression. · PADI Tec 50 · To 50 m
Tec TrimixHelium in the bottom mix. Takes you past the limits of air with a clear head. · PADI Tec Trimix · To 75 m
Rebreather / CCRClosed circuit. Quieter, warmer and far longer bottom times – against a much higher standard of routine. · PADI Rebreather / Type R · To 40 m

Where should you start?

Already hold technical certifications, or come from another training agency? Get in touch and we will work out what needs topping up.
