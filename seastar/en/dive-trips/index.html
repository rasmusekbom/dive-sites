Seastar / Trips

Dive trips

We have our own dive boat and run trips in the Stockholm archipelago and the Baltic through the season.

The Baltic is brackish, cold and dark – and precisely because of that, full of wood that does not rot. Shipworm cannot survive in this water, which is why the Baltic holds some of the best preserved wooden wrecks in the world.

Trips are set by the weather, the visibility and who is coming along. Newly certified, you go with a guide. A technical diver, and we plan the dive together.

Season, dates and prices vary. Call or email and we will tell you what is coming up next.

The dive club

Diving is better with people. The club is how we make sure there is somebody to dive with even when no course is running.

Dive travel

We organise trips to warmer water. A good way to do your first dives after certification in thirty metres of visibility, before you meet the Baltic in October.

Start with a conversation

Tell us where you are and we will suggest a route. No experience required to pick up the phone.
