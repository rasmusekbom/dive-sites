About us

With us you become a star in the water – a Seastar, simply put.

We are a PADI 5 Star IDC Dive Center in Skrubba in southern Stockholm. That means two things: that we teach all the way up to instructor, and that we are judged on how satisfied our students actually are – not only on whether we follow the standards.

We teach both sport diving and technical diving, and we help you with equipment for every environment. From a shorty in blue water and white sand in the tropics to a dry suit and remarkable wreck diving in the Baltic.

Our vision is to offer courses, experiences, equipment and travel that stand out.

The team

Ten PADI instructors, covering everything from entry-level courses to instructor training and technical diving.

Languages

We run courses in Swedish, English, Danish and Norwegian.
