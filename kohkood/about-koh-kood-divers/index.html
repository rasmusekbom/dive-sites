About us

Small on purpose

Koh Kood Divers has been family-run from Klong Chao since 2008. We are a handful of instructors, one boat, and a lot of time spent on the same reefs.

Bob opened the shop in 2008, when Koh Kood had a fraction of the resorts it has now and the reefs were known to almost nobody. Jessie joined a year later. The idea was simple and has not changed: small groups, good equipment, guides who know every coral head, and a day that feels like a day out with friends rather than a production line.

We are a PADI 5-Star Dive Centre, which means we meet PADI's standards for training, equipment and safety and are audited on them. We also teach SSI on request. Between us the team teaches in English, German, French, Dutch and Spanish.

We have stayed deliberately small. One boat means we know exactly who is on it. Four divers per guide means nobody is at the back of a line. It also means we fill up in high season, so book ahead.

Immerse yourself in crystal-clear water and explore the vibrant coral reefs surrounding Koh Kood. Dive alongside colourful tropical fish and, with a little luck, sea turtles — and let the underwater world create memories that last a lifetime.

Safety first at our ocean adventure centre

When it comes to your ocean adventure, we understand that safety is your top priority. Every trip has oxygen and a first-aid kit on board and at the shop. Equipment is Aqualung and Scubapro, serviced on schedule. Dive sites are chosen on the morning of the trip based on weather, sea state, visibility and who is on board — never by a fixed schedule that forces a dive in poor conditions.

Our instructors are PADI-certified with years of experience on these specific reefs, and highly knowledgeable. Courses follow PADI standards exactly: depth limits, ratios, skill sequences. Insurance is included in every course and every fun dive. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced diver, you are in capable hands.

The boat

A spacious dive boat with shade, a toilet, a freshwater shower, a proper kitchen for lunch, and room for gear without stepping over each other. It leaves from Bang Bao bay on the west coast, sheltered in most conditions.

Why Koh Kood

The island sits in the eastern Gulf of Thailand near the Cambodian border, far from the big-ticket dive destinations. That is the point. The sea is sheltered, the water is 27–30 °C year-round, the reefs are shallow and healthy, and on most days you will not see another dive boat. Come and experience the best the ocean has to offer with a team that has been here since 2008.

About us

Why divers choose us

Prime location

Our 5-star PADI centre sits in Klong Chao, in the heart of Koh Kood, with the boat leaving from sheltered Bang Bao bay. Most resorts are minutes away, and we take pride in our facilities and service.

Comfort and personal service

Never more than four divers with one guide or four students with one instructor. Our friendly staff help with anything from courses and equipment to tips for the rest of your stay on the island.

Safety standards

Oxygen and first aid on the boat and at the shop, well-maintained Aqualung and Scubapro equipment, conservative profiles, courses run to rigorous PADI standards and sites chosen daily for the conditions.

Any level, five languages

From an eight-year-old's first bubbles to Divemaster training, taught in English, German, French, Dutch or Spanish. Expert training from beginner to professional.
