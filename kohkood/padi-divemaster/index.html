PADI Divemaster Course

Go pro on a quiet island with a small, multilingual team. 4–8 weeks, 40–100 dives guaranteed.

Duration4–8 weeks+
Dives40–100
Min. age18
GroupMax 4 per instructor

Your first professional rating: certified to lead certified divers on dives, assist instructors and conduct some programmes yourself. We prioritise quality over quantity — fewer Divemasters trained well, not a conveyor belt — so the programme is flexible: complete it in as little as six weeks if you commit, or spread it across a season.

You'll dive a lot: 40 to 100 dives guaranteed around Koh Kood, Koh Rang and the Koh Chang wreck, with real customers on our boat, in a team where every instructor teaches in at least three languages. We may not be the cheapest option; the training is worth it.

How it works

  1. Dive theoryPhysics, physiology, equipment, the environment, decompression theory and the Recreational Dive Planner — to the level where you can explain it to others.
  2. Water skills and staminaSwims, tows, the equipment exchange, and the 20 demonstration-quality skills, performed to PADI Divemaster standard.
  3. Leadership and practical experienceMapping a site, briefings, assisting on real courses, guiding real customers, and the Discover Scuba Diving Leader internship.

Beyond the standards

Every Divemaster candidate here also does a workshop on the psychology of teaching diving, based on Marc Luxen's Dive Professional's Guide to Practical Psychology. You learn to recognise and correct the mistakes beginners make — not just manage them.

Divemaster Intensive — 4 weeks

Short on time? The intensive package gets you to Divemaster in four weeks of full-time training. Contact us for the schedule and to secure a place.

Career opportunities

As one of the leading centres on Koh Kood we regularly have openings for qualified Divemasters. Graduates are well placed to join our own team — leading dives, assisting instructors and sharing the reef with guests in a supportive, professional environment.

Not yet Rescue certified?

The Divemaster Internship has packages that include the courses you still need, and Zero to Hero covers everything from your first breath if you are starting from scratch.

Prerequisites

  • Age 18+
  • Open Water, Advanced and Rescue (or equivalents)
  • EFR within 2 years
  • Minimum 40 logged dives
  • Medical statement signed by a physician within 12 months

Frequently asked

What is a PADI Divemaster?

The first professional level in scuba diving. It lets you supervise certified divers and assist instructors during training. Our programme combines theory and real dive experience to prepare you for a professional career.

How long does it take?

4–8 weeks depending on availability and commitment. A 4-week intensive option exists for divers with limited time; the flexible structure lets you progress at your own pace.

How many dives do you need?

At least 40 logged dives plus Open Water, Advanced and Rescue to start. During the programme you complete 40 to 100 more around Koh Kood, Koh Rang and the wreck.

What does it take to become a Divemaster?

Be 18 or over, in good health, hold Open Water, Advanced and Rescue, have 40 logged dives and a current medical. The course itself covers theory, water skills, stamina tests and practical leadership training.

How much does a Divemaster earn in Thailand?

Typically from around 30,000 THB a month, rising with experience and workload. At Koh Kood Divers our Divemasters earn between 30,000 and 70,000 THB a month depending on experience, performance and season.

Is accommodation included?

No, but we can point you to long-stay options on the island at candidate rates.

Is there a discount on equipment?

Yes — if you plan to work afterwards we recommend buying your own gear, and we offer it at reduced prices during the programme.
