PADI Rescue Diver Course

Learn to prevent and manage problems in the water. Challenging, rewarding, and the course most divers say changed how they dive.

Duration2–3 days
Dives4
Min. age12
GroupMax 4 per instructor

The most important course for divers who want to be safer, more confident and more aware underwater. Rescue focuses on self-rescue, diver awareness and assisting others in an emergency: you learn to spot problems before they happen, manage stress — yours and others' — and handle a tired, panicked or unresponsive diver on the surface and underwater.

It's demanding and it's a lot of fun: scenarios, role-play, and instructors who enjoy staging surprises. Koh Kood's calm water, warm temperatures and good visibility let you focus on the skills rather than fight the conditions.

How it works

  1. Knowledge developmentPADI eLearning or classroom sessions: stress management, emergency planning, accident prevention and the psychology of panic — so you can respond calmly and effectively in real situations.
  2. Rescue skills practiceTen rescue exercises in shallow, controlled water: tows, unresponsive diver, in-water rescue breathing, exits, equipment removal. Instructors guide you closely.
  3. Rescue scenariosRealistic open-water scenarios that combine everything — building confidence, teamwork and leadership. Many divers call this the most valuable course they ever took.

What's included

  • Professional PADI instructor
  • Rescue skills training and scenario-based exercises
  • Full scuba equipment and boat trips to the training sites
  • Training materials and insurance
  • Small-group instruction

Who it is for

Certified Advanced Open Water divers (or equivalent) who want to be safer and more confident — whether you plan to continue to Divemaster or simply want to be a better dive buddy.

After Rescue

Many divers continue their professional journey with the Divemaster course here on Koh Kood, the first step towards becoming a dive professional.

Prerequisites

  • PADI Advanced Open Water or equivalent (PADI, NAUI…)
  • Age 15+ (Junior Rescue from 12)
  • Emergency First Responder (CPR and first aid) within the last 2 years — not certified? Let us know and take the EFR course in one day just before Rescue
  • Good health

Frequently asked

Is Rescue physically hard?

It is the most physical of the recreational courses: tows, lifts and exits. Ordinary fitness is enough.

Is it required for Divemaster?

Yes, along with EFR and 40 logged dives.
