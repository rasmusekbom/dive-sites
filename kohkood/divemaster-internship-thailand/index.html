Divemaster Internship in Thailand

Divemaster plus the courses you still need, plus real teaching practice. Packages from any level, with or without your own equipment.

Duration4–8 weeks+
Dives40–100
Min. age18
GroupMax 4 per instructor

Where most dive schools stop at assisting courses and leading dives, we focus heavily on teaching skills. In our internship you learn to recognise and correct the mistakes beginner divers make — not just manage them. The programme includes an integrated workshop on the psychology of teaching diving, based on Marc Luxen's Dive Professional's Guide to Practical Psychology, and the PADI Discover Scuba Diving Leader Internship — so you graduate able to conduct the skills portion of DSD programmes, something most Divemaster graduates simply cannot do.

We know you want to make a few dives before starting, so the programme opens with a few days of fun diving until you are comfortable with the reefs around Koh Kood. Over four to eight weeks you then train as a Divemaster in a working dive centre: you dive regularly, learn the craft properly and gain the experience that only comes from doing the job day after day. You have a mentor throughout, a SIM card with unlimited internet, and everything arranged from the moment you land in Bangkok.

More than a Divemaster course

Most Divemaster courses teach you to guide certified divers and assist instructors. Ours also teaches the teaching side: how to brief a skill clearly, observe students underwater and evaluate their performance — foundations of instructor training that are not part of a normal Divemaster course. In a typical programme you demonstrate a skill after the instructor has taught it; here you teach it yourself, watch the student carefully, and judge whether they performed it correctly and, more importantly, safely.

The human side of diving

How people think and respond underwater, how to read stress, how to manage a group with different personalities and backgrounds, and how to lead with confidence. Theory is taught in person, not just via eLearning. You finish with more knowledge, stronger skills and far more confidence than a typical Divemaster candidate — and if you go on to your Instructor course, you arrive ahead of the pack.

Half-days

Training is organised around half-days, leaving the rest of the day free for your own activities: working remotely, exploring the island, or joining one of the optional Muay Thai or yoga packages.

What you will do

The programme covers every dimension of professional Divemaster work — underwater skills and rescue, theory, guiding, conducting programmes, and the psychology of leading and teaching divers.

  1. 01
    Psychology of leading and teaching
    • How divers think and respond underwater — and what that means for you
    • Recognising and managing stress in students and guided divers
    • Observation skills: what to watch for and how to read a diver
    • Balancing control and autonomy — leading without micromanaging
    • Cultural and individual differences in learning and communication
    • Based on Dr Marc Luxen's Dive Professional's Guide to Practical Psychology, part of your reading material
  2. 02
    Conducting Divemaster programmes
    • Discover Scuba Diving (confined water and open water)
    • ReActivate / Scuba Review for certified divers
    • Brief, supervise and evaluate student performance
  3. 03
    Briefing and guiding divers
    • Site selection, equipment setup and diver check-in
    • Emergency equipment preparation
    • Lead and monitor open-water dives with real divers
  4. 04
    Teaching skills — beyond the standard
    • Brief and demonstrate skills to students
    • Observe and evaluate student performance underwater
    • Foundations of PADI instructor-level teaching methodology
  5. 05
    Diving theory
    • Dive environment
    • Physics and physiology
    • Equipment
    • Decompression theory and the RDP
  6. 06
    Skills circuit and equipment exchange
    • Scuba skills demonstrated and evaluated
    • Full equipment exchange with a buddy while neutrally buoyant
    • Performed to PADI Divemaster standard
  7. 07
    Deep dive scenario
    • Emergency breathing equipment setup
    • Compass navigation at depth
    • Controlled ascent and 3-minute safety stop with no reference line
  8. 08
    Search and recovery workshop
    • Methodical underwater search patterns
    • Underwater knots: bowline, two half-hitches, sheet bend
    • Lift-bag rigging and object recovery
  9. 09
    Rescue skills and assessment
    • Locate and surface a submerged unresponsive diver
    • In-water rescue breathing and tow
    • Full exit and emergency assistance plan
  10. 10
    Snorkelling supervision and stamina
    • Conduct the Advanced Snorkeler course
    • 400 m swim · 15 min tread water · 800 m snorkel · 100 m diver tow
  11. 11
    Underwater naturalist dive
    • Applied marine ecology on the reef
    • Species identification and environmental awareness
  12. 12
    DSD Leader certification
    • Included as standard: teach the confined-water skills of Discover Scuba Diving independently as a Divemaster
    • Four DSDs conducted under supervision
  13. 13
    Mapping
    • Survey a real dive site with a buddy
    • Produce a detailed map: depths, relief, hazards, entry and exit points, points of interest, facilities

Frequently asked

How long is the internship?

Four to eight weeks for Divemaster alone; longer if the package includes Rescue or earlier courses.

Do I pay for fun dives during the internship?

No — 40 to 100 dives are included and guaranteed.

What if I am not Rescue certified yet?

Choose the package that starts from your current level — from Rescue & EFR, or from Advanced + Rescue & EFR. The programme adapts to your experience and builds from there.

How do I apply?

Message us with your certification level, dive count, rough dates and how long you can stay. Everyone has different plans and needs; we will explain how to make this fit yours.
