Special & Wreck Dives from Koh Kood

Full-day trips towards Koh Chang for experienced divers. Wrecks to 30 m. Run when conditions allow.

DurationFull day · 8 AM – 5 PM
Dives2
Min. age10
GroupMax 4 per guide

Beyond the regular reef sites we occasionally run longer trips: full days towards Koh Chang, where wrecks sit at up to 30 metres. Instead of staying close to Koh Kood, these trips depart directly from the island and give certified divers deeper profiles, longer boat journeys and carefully planned dives.

These trips have no fixed schedule. We plan each one individually when the sea state, visibility and the group make sense — a flexible approach that lets us pick the best possible day for safety, comfort and enjoyment.

How it works

  1. Message usTell us your dates, certification level and roughly how many dives you have.
  2. We check conditionsForecast, swell, visibility and who else wants to go. We confirm the day when it looks right.
  3. Full day outDepart 8 AM, two dives with a long surface interval and lunch, back around 5 PM.

What makes these trips different

Unlike standard reef dives, special trips are about variety and exploration. They require more detailed planning, suitable weather and sea conditions, and certified, experienced divers. Briefings are more detailed, groups stay small, and profiles are adjusted to the experience of everyone on board — so you get more advanced dives without pressure or rush.

Wreck diving near Koh Chang

On selected days the trip includes wreck diving near Koh Chang, where certain wrecks reach depths of up to 30 metres. The wrecks are not at Koh Kood itself, but they are reachable on a full-day boat trip when conditions allow. Because of the depth and the more complex environment, these dives are for certified divers with sufficient experience, comfort at depth and recent diving activity.

Safety and planning

  • Weather forecasts
  • Sea state and swell
  • Visibility and current
  • Diver experience and comfort level

Sites are selected on the day against all four. If conditions are not suitable the trip is adjusted or postponed — no exceptions. Small groups mean personal attention, clear communication and a relaxed pace, so even deeper and longer dives stay enjoyable.

Who it suits

  • Certified divers who want deeper or more varied profiles
  • Anyone interested in wreck diving near Koh Chang
  • Small groups or private trips
  • Divers who prefer careful planning over a rush

Prerequisites

  • Scuba certification with recent dives
  • Comfortable at depth (Advanced recommended for 30 m wrecks)
  • Minimum age 10

Frequently asked

When is the best time for the Koh Chang wreck?

November through April, when the sea is calmer, visibility clearer and the weather stable. We only schedule wreck trips in the main season.

Do I need a wreck speciality?

No. The dives stay outside the wreck. If you want to learn penetration, ask about the PADI Wreck Diver speciality.

How many people go?

We keep it to a small group. If nobody else is booked, we can quote a private trip.

Are there snorkelling or diving services at the wreck?

Guided wreck dives are part of our fun-dive programme as a 5-star PADI centre, with experienced instructors and an organised boat trip. Snorkellers are better served by our reef trips — the wreck is too deep to see from the surface.

Can I rent equipment for the wreck trip?

A full equipment set is included in the price. Our staff will help select the right gear; everything is serviced and maintained for safety and comfort.
