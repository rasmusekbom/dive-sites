Bookings & cancellations

1. Non-refundable discounted rate (10%)

We offer a 10% special discounted rate, which is non-refundable. Due to our location near the Thai–Cambodian border, bookings made under this discounted rate cannot be cancelled or refunded, provided that Koh Kood is officially safe to visit and operating normally. Koh Kood remains in a safe zone, with normal daily operations and no impact on guest activities or travel. If official Thai authorities restrict travel to Koh Kood or close the island, guests will be offered a date change, credit for future use, or a full refund.

2. Non-refundable fees & materials

The following costs are strictly non-refundable once issued or paid: bank or transfer fees; PADI eLearning, manuals, certification fees or training materials; any third-party fees already paid on behalf of the guest.

3. Safety & travel status

The safety status of Koh Kood is determined solely by official announcements from Thai government authorities. Personal travel concerns, airline changes or media reports do not qualify for refunds.

4. Flexible booking option (refundable)

Guests who prefer a flexible cancellation option (refund available up to 24 hours before the scheduled activity) are advised to book directly with us via email or WhatsApp. This flexible option is not available for the 10% discounted non-refundable rate.

5. Acceptance of terms

To proceed with the 10% discounted rate, guests must confirm their agreement to these terms by checking the box at checkout. If guests do not agree to the 10% discount conditions, they are advised to contact us directly via email or WhatsApp to arrange an alternative booking option. By completing a booking with Koh Kood Divers, guests confirm that they have read, understood and accepted these terms.

Questions? Message us on WhatsApp or email booking@kohkooddivers.com.
