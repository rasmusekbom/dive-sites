PADI Advanced Open Water Diver

Five adventure dives, minimal theory. Deep and navigation are set; pick three more. Certifies you to 30 m.

Duration2–3 days
Dives5
Min. age12
GroupMax 4 per instructor

The fun course. In just two or three days you complete five adventure dives, each with one new skill, and almost no classroom. Deep and underwater navigation are mandatory; you choose three more from fifteen options — wreck, drift, night, peak performance buoyancy, photography, naturalist, fish ID and so on.

Compared with Open Water this course is fun-focused with minimal theory. Once certified you're cleared to 30 metres, which opens up the Koh Chang wrecks and most of the world's best sites.

How it works

  1. Choose your divesMeet your instructor and talk through your preferences to pick your three electives. Complete the matching chapters and knowledge reviews in the Adventures in Diving manual — we can send the PADI eBook for pre-course reading at home.
  2. Day 1: two divesPickup between 8:15 AM and 8:50 AM, drive to Bang Bao bay, dive briefing and plan for the day at the centre. At the site around 10 AM, two adventure dives with lunch in between, then knowledge review and debrief with your instructor. Back on Koh Kood between 2:15 PM and 3 PM.
  3. Day 2: three divesA similar day with a third dive — possibly a night dive, in which case we pick you up from your resort around 5:30 PM and return around 7:30 PM. Back at the dive school you finish the knowledge reviews and your Advanced certification is processed.

Deep and navigation

The deep dive takes you to 30 metres on a Koh Rang pinnacle or the wreck, and teaches you how depth changes colour, air consumption and your thinking. Navigation is compass and natural navigation over the reef — the skill that makes you independent.

Loved it?

Continue with the PADI Rescue Diver course (2–3 days): dive safety, accident prevention and response — the course most divers say changed how they dive, and the one that makes you a true hero on the boat.

Prerequisites

  • PADI Open Water Diver or equivalent (SSI, NAUI…)
  • Age 15+ (Junior Advanced from 12)
  • Able to swim, good health

Frequently asked

How soon after Open Water can I do Advanced?

Immediately. Many people do them back to back over five or six days.

Is there a wreck dive option?

Yes, when we can run a Koh Chang wreck trip in the same period. Otherwise choose another elective and do wreck later.

Is there an exam?

No exam — just knowledge reviews for each of your five dives.
