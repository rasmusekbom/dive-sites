PADI Open Water Diver Course in Koh Kood

The world's most recognised dive certification. Skills in a shallow bay, then four open-water dives to 18 m.

Duration3–4 days
Dives4
Min. age10
GroupMax 4 per instructor

The certification that lets you dive anywhere in the world, to 18 metres, with a buddy. Over three or four days you learn the theory, practise skills in a calm shallow bay, and complete four dives on the reefs around Koh Kood.

Our goal is divers who have genuinely mastered the theory and the practical skills, so that every dive afterwards is enjoyable and safe. We keep groups small — never more than four students per instructor — and Koh Kood's calm, shallow, uncrowded sites make it one of the best places in Thailand to learn. Dives average around 10 metres, last 45–65 minutes depending on your air, and end with lunch on the boat.

How it works

  1. Before you arrive: eLearningThe PADI Open Water code is included in the course. Study on the PADI app on your phone at home or on the plane, so your days here are spent in the water — and you have time for excursions and extra dives after certification.
  2. Day 1: theory check and confined waterWe pick you up between 8 and 9 at your resort. Your instructor assesses your theory, answers questions and familiarises you with the equipment. After a full briefing you start confined-water training in a shallow bay: setting up equipment, buddy checks and the essential skills.
  3. Day 2: open-water dives 1 & 2From the boat. Each dive starts with a few skills from your confined-water training, but most of the time is spent exploring the reef. Lunch on board and a rest before the second dive.
  4. Day 3: open-water dives 3 & 4Two more dives, to a maximum of 18 metres, fine-tuning your skills among fish, coral and marine life. Sites are 45–80 minutes away; we return to Bang Bao beach around 3 PM, review with your instructor, and your certification is processed the same day.

What you learn

Equipment and how to look after it, how pressure affects your body, dive planning and the PADI tables or computer, buoyancy control, buddy procedures, and how to handle the small problems that come up underwater — calmly and without fuss.

Safety and comfort

Insurance is included. Dive sites are chosen each day for calm conditions. There is oxygen and first aid on the boat, a toilet and a shower, and the crew is used to nervous first-timers.

Certified — what now?

You can now explore dive sites worldwide, rent equipment and dive to 18 metres. Most people add a couple of fun dives to their stay. If you have two or three more days, the Advanced Open Water course takes you to 30 metres with five adventure dives and almost no theory — or complete individual Adventure Dives that credit towards Advanced later.

What you'll see

Your training dives are real reef dives: coral gardens, clownfish, angelfish, parrotfish, moray eels, and often a turtle on dive three or four.

Prerequisites

  • Age 15+ (Junior Open Water from 10)
  • Able to swim: 200 m any style (or 300 m with mask, snorkel and fins) and float or tread water for 10 minutes, no time limit
  • Good health (medical questionnaire)

Frequently asked

What is PADI Open Water?

The world's most popular beginner certification and the first level of the PADI system. It lets you dive independently with a buddy to 18 metres, combines theory, confined-water practice and four open-water dives, and the certification is for life and recognised worldwide.

How many dives do you need?

Four open-water dives, after practising the essential skills in confined water. The four dives are where you apply what you learned in a safe, controlled environment.

How far do you have to swim?

200 metres without stopping, any style, no time limit — or 300 metres with mask, snorkel and fins. You also float or tread water for 10 minutes to show basic comfort in the water.

How much does PADI Open Water cost in Thailand?

Generally between 13,000 and 18,000 THB depending on the centre, group size and what is included. With us the course is 15,500 THB with all materials, equipment, insurance and certification included; the only extra is the 400 THB marine park fee on Koh Rang days.

How long does the course take?

Three days if you have done the eLearning before you arrive, four if you do the theory here.

Is there an exam?

Yes, a multiple-choice knowledge review at the end of the eLearning. Your instructor goes through anything you got wrong.

What is the minimum age?

10 for Junior Open Water (depth limits apply until 15), 15 for the full certification.

Do I need my own equipment?

No, everything is included: wetsuit, BCD, regulator, computer, mask, fins.

Is availability limited?

In high season, yes. Confirm your spot in advance — WhatsApp is the fastest way and we will answer personally.
