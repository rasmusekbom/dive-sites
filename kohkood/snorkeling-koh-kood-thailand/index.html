Snorkelling in Koh Kood

Three sites, 45 minutes each, lunch in between. Suitable for all ages — even non-swimmers with a vest.

Duration1 day
Dives3 snorkel stops
GroupMax 4 per guide

Snorkelling is one of the best things to do on Koh Kood: crystal-clear water teeming with fish and vibrant coral gardens, a short boat ride from shore. We use the same boat and the same reefs as the divers, seen from the surface.

We stop at three sites for about 45 minutes each with lunch on board in between. The mild current makes it easy and enjoyable even for non-swimmers — the boat carries life jackets, life rings and ropes, and the crew keeps an eye on everyone.

Koh Kood trips — Mon, Wed, Fri, Sun

Depart 8 AM, a short and comfortable drive to Bang Bao, then a maximum of 45 minutes by boat to the reefs around Koh Kood. Three snorkel stops of about 45 minutes each, lunch on board (vegetarian on request), back mid-afternoon. 1,000 THB per person including hotel transfers, boat, equipment, lunch and drinks.

Koh Rang Marine Park — Tue, Thu, Sat

About 90 minutes by boat to the protected islands of Koh Rang. Three snorkel sites with the healthiest coral in the area. 1,200 THB per person plus the national park fee (200 THB adults, 100 THB kids, 40 THB Thai nationals), paid in cash to the rangers on the boat.

Want to go under?

If you're curious about what's below the surface, Discover Scuba Diving lets you try a real dive with an instructor on the same boat. Many snorkellers switch halfway through their holiday.

What you'll see

Shallow coral gardens with clownfish, parrotfish, butterflyfish and sergeant majors right below you. At Koh Rang the coral is denser and turtles are seen regularly.

Prerequisites

  • No experience needed
  • Life jackets, life rings and ropes on every trip
  • Kids welcome (park fee 100 THB)

Frequently asked

Can you snorkel in Koh Kood?

Yes — clear, warm water and healthy reefs a short boat ride from shore. Most of the best spots are reached by boat, which is why we run guided day trips.

Is Koh Kood good for snorkelling?

It is considered one of the best places for snorkelling in eastern Thailand. Clean water, limited development and much less boat traffic than the busier islands keep visibility high and the reefs healthy.

Where are the best snorkelling spots?

Around the small islands off Koh Kood on local days, and in Koh Rang Marine Park on park days. The guide picks the three best sites for the conditions.

Do I need experience?

No. Beginners, families and non-swimmers with a life jacket are all fine. Masks, snorkels, fins and jackets are provided and the guide helps throughout.

Are there sharks in Koh Kood?

Shark encounters are extremely rare around the snorkelling sites. The reefs are calm and safe; what you will see is small reef fish, coral and other harmless marine life.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, towel, reef-safe sunscreen, a hat for the boat. We provide mask, snorkel, fins, lunch and drinks.
