Discover Scuba Diving in Koh Kood

Your first breaths underwater with an instructor beside you. Two real ocean dives, no exam, no commitment.

Duration1 day
Dives1–2
Min. age8
GroupMax 4 per instructor

The easiest and safest way to find out if diving is for you. No classroom, no exam: a briefing on the boat, a few minutes practising in shallow water, and then a real dive on the reef under the direct supervision of a PADI instructor who holds position next to you the whole time.

Koh Kood is one of the best places in Thailand for a first dive. The sea is calm, the water is clear and warm, the sites are shallow and the reefs are uncrowded. Most people are surprised how quickly it feels normal.

How it works

  1. Briefing and equipment on the boatMeet your instructor at the dive boat. How the equipment works, how to breathe and equalise, how to communicate underwater, and a full safety briefing so you feel relaxed before you get in.
  2. First dive in calm waterStraight off the boat at a calm, shallow site. Because conditions are gentle and visibility is usually very good, most beginners feel comfortable within minutes.
  3. Lunch on the boatA relaxed break with a view of the Koh Kood coastline. Rest, hydrate, and get ready for round two.
  4. Second dive at another reefA different site, with your instructor again at your side while you explore coral, tropical fish and the marine life that makes Koh Kood special. Most people want this one.
  5. Back to Bang BaoCruise back to Koh Kood around 3 PM and we drive you home. Two dives, and your first day as a diver.

Two dives, double the confidence

Each dive builds familiarity with the equipment and the feeling of breathing underwater. The second dive is where it usually clicks — which is why the two-dive option is the one we recommend.

Counts towards certification

If your curiosity is ignited, the day can count as the first dive of your PADI Open Water course, so nothing is wasted. Families with younger kids can look at the Junior Open Water course from age 10.

Safety

Discover Scuba follows official PADI standards: a maximum depth of 12 metres (usually much less), an instructor within arm's reach, and never more than four beginners per instructor. Oxygen and first aid are on the boat and insurance is included.

What you'll see

Clownfish in their anemones, angelfish, butterflyfish, parrotfish, moray eels peering out of the coral, nudibranchs, colourful coral formations — and quite often a turtle. The reefs are part of a protected marine ecosystem, which keeps them clean and full of life.

Prerequisites

  • No experience or certification needed — just bring your curiosity
  • Minimum age 8
  • Good general health (medical questionnaire on the day)

Frequently asked

What is PADI Discover Scuba?

An introductory experience for beginners who want to try diving without committing to a full certification course. You dive under the direct supervision of a PADI professional after learning the basic safety rules and skills.

How deep is the Discover Scuba dive?

The programme allows a maximum of 12 metres, but the actual depth depends on your comfort and the instructor. Usually 5–8 metres — the best reef is shallow anyway.

How long does it take?

About half a day in the water: a short briefing, shallow-water practice and at least one open-water dive. With lunch and the second dive it is a full, relaxed day out.

Can a Divemaster teach Discover Scuba?

No. Only a certified PADI Instructor can conduct the programme. A Divemaster may assist under the direct supervision of an instructor.

Is it a course? Do I get a licence?

It is an experience, not a certification — it does not result in a permanent diving licence. It does count towards Open Water if you continue.

Do I need to be able to swim?

You should be comfortable in the water. You do not need to be a strong swimmer; the equipment keeps you buoyant.

Can I do it with glasses or contacts?

Soft contact lenses are fine. We have a few prescription masks — ask when booking.

Can I fly the next day?

Wait at least 18 hours after a single dive and 24 hours after two before flying.
