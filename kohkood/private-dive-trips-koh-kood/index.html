Private Dive & Snorkel Trips

The boat, the guide and the plan to yourselves. Diving, snorkelling or both.

Duration1 day
DivesYou decide
GroupMax 4 per guide

Sometimes you want the boat to yourselves: a family with a mix of divers and snorkellers, a group of friends, a photographer who wants an hour on one coral head, or a couple who would rather not share the day with strangers.

On a private trip the plan is yours. Dive profiles, surface intervals, sites and timing are adapted to who is on board and to the sea on the day. Snorkellers and divers share the boat comfortably — while divers are down, snorkellers explore the reef above.

Private scuba diving

For certified divers who want flexibility and personal attention: slower pace, more time underwater, sites chosen around your interests. Photographers in particular get a lot out of it.

Private snorkelling

For non-divers, families and anyone who prefers the surface. Calm conditions and shallow reefs make snorkelling around Koh Kood easy, and with a private boat you choose the spots and the timing.

Mixed groups

Couples, families or friends where some dive and some snorkel. Everyone enjoys the same reef at the same time, nobody has to compromise.

Planning and safety

Private trips are planned like every other trip: sites chosen on the day for calm conditions, and adjusted or rescheduled if the sea is not right. Flexibility is the whole point, so we use it.

Prerequisites

  • Any level — divers, snorkellers or a mix
  • Certification card for divers
  • Book a few days ahead so we can plan the boat

Frequently asked

What does a private trip cost?

It depends on the number of people, dives and the destination. Message us with your group size and dates and we will quote within the day.

Can we choose Koh Rang on a non-park day?

Usually yes, conditions permitting. The park fee applies.

Can we combine a course with a private trip?

Yes — Discover Scuba for the beginners in the group while certified divers do fun dives is a popular combination.
