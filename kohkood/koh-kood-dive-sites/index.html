Where we dive

Dive sites around Koh Kood

Shallow, healthy and uncrowded. Most sites sit between 10 and 18 metres, Koh Rang pinnacles reach around 20, and the wreck near Koh Chang goes to 30. Sites are chosen each morning for the conditions.

Tue · Thu · Sat

Koh Rang Marine Park

A protected cluster of islands 90 minutes west. No fishing, no anchoring, dense fish life and the best visibility in the area.

Hin Rap

5–18 m

A long submerged reef with coral gardens on both sides and schools of fusiliers and snappers in the blue. Mild current, easy navigation — a Koh Rang favourite for training dives and first-timers in the park.

Hin Kuak Ma

8–20 m

A rocky pinnacle dropping to around 20 metres, covered in soft coral and sea fans. Batfish, barracuda and the occasional ray in the deeper section; nudibranchs everywhere on the rock.

Koh Yak

5–16 m

Two small islands with a shallow, sheltered reef between them. Turtles are seen regularly here. Perfect for long, slow dives and for snorkellers on the same trip.

Koh Thong Lang

5–14 m

Shallow hard-coral slopes with dense reef fish: parrotfish, angelfish, butterflyfish, sergeant majors. Bright, easy and photogenic.

Hin Sam Sao

10–22 m

Three pinnacles rising from the sand, the deepest Koh Rang site. Sea fans, soft coral, moray eels and occasionally larger schools in the current. For divers comfortable at 20 metres.

Mon · Wed · Fri · Sun

Koh Kood local reefs

The islands and bays around Koh Kood itself, 45–80 minutes from the pier. Relaxed coral gardens, ideal for training and easy fun dives.

Koh Mai Si

5–14 m

Small islands just off Koh Kood with a gentle coral slope from 5 to 14 metres. Our usual site for Open Water training dives and Discover Scuba — calm, shallow, full of clownfish.

Koh Raet

6–16 m

A sheltered reef on the west side of Koh Kood with hard coral, staghorn fields and plenty of macro life. A good second dive on local days.

Ao Phrao

3–12 m

A shallow bay used for confined-water sessions and as a fallback site when the wind picks up. Seagrass, juvenile fish and calm water — a good place to learn.

On request

Special trips

Full-day trips towards Koh Chang for experienced divers when conditions allow.

HTMS Chang wreck

18–30 m

A 100-metre former navy landing ship sunk in 2012 near Koh Chang as an artificial reef, sitting upright at around 30 metres. Now home to schools of batfish, barracuda and groupers. Advanced certification and recent experience required; special trips only.

Good to know

Conditions

Water temperature

27–30 °C all year. A 3 mm shorty or a rash guard is plenty.

Visibility

Typically 10–20 metres in season, best from November to April.

Current

Mild or none at most sites. Occasionally moderate at the Koh Rang pinnacles.

Season

November to April is the main season. We run through the year when conditions allow and pause when the monsoon makes it unsafe.

Depths and names to be confirmed by the shop; conditions vary by season.
