Why divers choose us

Small on purpose

Prime location

Our 5-star PADI centre sits in Klong Chao, in the heart of Koh Kood, with the boat leaving from sheltered Bang Bao bay. Most resorts are minutes away, and we take pride in our facilities and service.

Comfort and personal service

Never more than four divers with one guide or four students with one instructor. Our friendly staff help with anything from courses and equipment to tips for the rest of your stay on the island.

Safety standards

Oxygen and first aid on the boat and at the shop, well-maintained Aqualung and Scubapro equipment, conservative profiles, courses run to rigorous PADI standards and sites chosen daily for the conditions.

Any level, five languages

From an eight-year-old's first bubbles to Divemaster training, taught in English, German, French, Dutch or Spanish. Expert training from beginner to professional.

Good to know

Questions people ask

Is Koh Kood good for scuba diving?

Yes. The island sits in the sheltered eastern Gulf of Thailand, so the sea is usually calm, the water is 27–30 °C all year, and the reefs are shallow and healthy with good visibility. Tourism pressure is low compared with Koh Tao or Phuket, which means uncrowded sites and relaxed marine life. It suits beginners and easy-going certified divers particularly well.

When is the best time to dive Koh Kood?

November to April. The sea is calm, visibility is at its best and the water sits around 28–30 °C. We run trips through the season and pause when the monsoon makes conditions unsafe.

I've never dived. Can I still join?

Yes. Discover Scuba Diving needs no experience — you'll be with an instructor the whole time, in shallow, calm water. If you love it, the day counts towards your Open Water certification.

What marine life can I see?

Healthy hard and soft coral, schooling reef fish, parrotfish, angelfish and butterflyfish, moray eels, nudibranchs, rays resting on the sand, and turtles at the quieter sites. Large pelagics are rare; the reef life is the point.

How do I get to Koh Kood?

Fly to Bangkok or Trat, then a speedboat or ferry from Laem Sok pier (about 1–1.5 hours). We're happy to recommend boats and resorts — just message us.
