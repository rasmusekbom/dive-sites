PADI Scuba Diver Course

Half the Open Water course, a full certification to dive with a professional to 12 m. Upgrade later, anywhere.

Duration2–3 days
Dives2
Min. age10
GroupMax 4 per instructor

Short on days but want a real certification? The PADI Scuba Diver course is the first half of Open Water: the first three theory sections, three confined-water sessions and two ocean dives. You're certified to dive to 12 metres under the supervision of a PADI professional, anywhere in the world.

Koh Kood is Thailand's hidden gem for learning: quiet dive sites, healthy reefs, excellent visibility and instructors who guide you every step. When you have more time — here or at home — you complete the remaining dives and upgrade to a full Open Water Diver. Nothing is repeated.

How it works

  1. Knowledge developmentPADI eLearning before you arrive, or with your instructor here. Equipment, safety procedures, underwater communication and dive planning — a few hours in total.
  2. Confined-water trainingEquipment setup, buddy checks and the core skills in a calm, shallow bay, at your own pace, in a small group with personal attention.
  3. Two open-water divesFrom the boat, on coral reefs full of tropical fish, with lunch in between. You apply what you learned while enjoying the underwater beauty of Koh Kood.

Why learn on Koh Kood

  • Untouched dive sites — healthy coral, clear water and no crowds
  • Colourful reefs and marine life on every dive, so even training dives are memorable
  • A globally recognised PADI certification accepted at thousands of dive centres worldwide

Want to dive independently? Upgrade to Open Water

The next step after Scuba Diver is the Open Water course, which lets you dive to 18 metres with a buddy and without a professional. Many guests upgrade during their stay because conditions are calm, learning is easy and it only takes a few extra days — so you leave Koh Kood as a fully certified diver. If you have three or four days from the start, go straight for Open Water.

Prerequisites

  • Age 15+ (Junior Scuba Diver from 10)
  • Able to swim
  • Good health (medical questionnaire)

Frequently asked

Can I upgrade to Open Water later?

Yes, at any PADI centre in the world. You complete the remaining theory, confined sessions and two more ocean dives.

Is the certification recognised everywhere?

Yes, it is a full PADI certification with a lifetime card.
