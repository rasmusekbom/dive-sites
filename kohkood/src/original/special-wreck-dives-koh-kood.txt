## Special & Wreck Dives from Koh Kood
Exclusive Dive Trips – Available on Request
Wreck diving from Koh Kood gives certified divers the opportunity to explore dive sites beyond the island’s regular reef dives. Instead of staying close to Koh Kood, these special trips depart directly from the island and may include wreck diving sites near Koh Chang. As a result, divers enjoy deeper profiles, longer boat journeys, and carefully planned dives based on sea conditions and diver experience.
Because there are no fixed dates, each trip is planned individually. This flexible approach allows us to choose the best possible day to ensure safety, comfort, and enjoyment.
### What Makes Special Dive Trips Unique?
Unlike standard reef dives, special dive trips focus on variety and exploration.
For this reason, these trips require:
More detailed planning
Suitable weather and sea conditions
Certified and experienced divers
Therefore, dive briefings are more detailed, group sizes remain small, and dive profiles are adjusted to match the experience level of all participants. This approach allows divers to enjoy more advanced dives without pressure or rush.
### Wreck Diving Trips Near Koh Chang
On selected days, special dive trips from Koh Kood include wreck diving near Koh Chang, where certain wrecks reach depths of up to 30 metres. Although these wrecks are not located directly at Koh Kood, they are accessible as part of longer full-day boat trips when conditions allow.
Because wreck diving involves deeper depths and more complex environments, these dives are offered only to certified divers with sufficient experience, comfort at depth, and recent diving activity. Before confirming the trip, sea conditions are carefully checked to ensure safe travel and controlled underwater conditions.
### Safety & Dive Planning
At Koh Kood Divers, safety always comes first.
Therefore, dive sites are selected daily based on:
Weather forecasts
Sea state and swell
Visibility and currents
Diver experience and comfort level
If conditions are not suitable, trips are adjusted or postponed. In addition, small group sizes allow for personal attention, clear communication, and a relaxed dive pace. This ensures that even deeper or longer dives remain enjoyable and stress-free.
### Who Are These Special Dives For?
Special and wreck dives from Koh Kood are ideal for:
Certified divers looking for deeper or more varied dive profiles
Divers interested in wreck diving experiences near Koh Chang
Small groups or private dive trips
Divers who prefer careful planning and a relaxed approach
Overall, these trips are perfect for divers who want to expand their experience while maintaining safety and comfort.
