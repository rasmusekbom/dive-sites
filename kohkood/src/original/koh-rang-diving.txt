## Koh Rang Marine Park Diving – Protected Reef Dives Near Koh Kood
Koh Rang Marine Park diving offers some of the best relaxed reef dives in eastern Thailand. Located between Koh Kood and Koh Chang, this protected marine park is known for clear water, healthy coral reefs, and calm conditions. Because the area is protected from fishing and anchoring, the reefs remain vibrant and full of life, making Koh Rang ideal for fun diving, training dives, and underwater photography.
### Why Dive in Koh Rang Marine Park
Koh Rang Marine Park is perfect for divers who want long, easy dives in shallow water. In addition, visibility is often excellent, and currents are usually mild. As a result, divers can enjoy slow exploration of coral gardens and marine life without stress.
### Marine Life You Can See While Diving in Koh Rang
Divers regularly see parrotfish, butterflyfish, angelfish, moray eels, nudibranchs, and schools of reef fish. In addition, rays and turtles are occasionally spotted cruising over the reef. Because of the protection, fish life is abundant and relaxed around divers.

### Koh Chang Wreck Diving – Frequently Asked Questions
#### Best time of year to dive the Koh Chang wreck
The best time to dive the Koh Chang wreck is from November through April. During these months, the sea is generally calmer, visibility is clearer, and weather conditions are more stable, making it the ideal season for wreck diving in the area.
Koh Kood Divers schedules trips during the main diving season to ensure safe and enjoyable conditions.
