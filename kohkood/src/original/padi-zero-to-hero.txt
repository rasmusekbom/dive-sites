# Padi Zero to Hero
## PREREQUISITES AND INCLUSIONS
 Duration: 8 weeks +
### Prerequisites:
 Be at least 18 years old
 Have a signed medical statement valid within the last 12 months, stating you are fit for diving.
 Be in good health
### Included in the price:
 The PADI Divemaster Manual (in English or French or German or Dutch )
 The PADI Instructor Manual (in English or French or German or Dutch)
 PADI Scuba Tune-Up Guidebook
 Encyclopedia of Recreational Diving
 PADI Divemaster Diving Log Book
 The PADI Divemaster waterproof slates
 Assistance from English, French, German and Dutch
 Theory and practical lessons
 Log book
 Between 40 and 100 dives guaranteed on the dive sites around koh kood, koh Rang and the wreck close to koh Chang
 Complete set of diving equipment for the duration of the internship (and possibility to buy personal diving equipment at a reduced price if you are looking to work after you finish the training this is recomended)
 Tanks and weights
 Drinks during day trip excursions
 Oxygen Kit and First Aid available on site
 PADI Divemaster certification fee

## Your Ultimate Diving Journey with Koh Kood Divers
 Welcome to Koh Kood Divers, where we turn your passion for diving into a lifelong adventure. Our PADI Zero to Hero program is designed for those who dream of taking their first breaths underwater and eventually becoming a certified diving professional. From zero experience to becoming a PADI Divemaster, we’re here to guide you every step of the way.
### Why Choose PADI Zero to Hero with Koh Kood Divers?
Immersive Learning Experience: Our meticulously designed program delves deep into the world of PADI courses, ensuring you receive a comprehensive and immersive education in scuba diving.
Guided by Diving Maestros: Our seasoned PADI instructors don’t just teach; they inspire and mentor. Drawing from their extensive experience, they guide you towards becoming a proficient and confident diver.
Exploration Unveiled: Koh Kood’s underwater landscapes are your training ground. From vibrant coral gardens to intricate marine ecosystems, each dive is a canvas for honing your skills and broadening your knowledge.
Transition to a Diving Career: Completing the PADI Zero to Hero program opens doors to a variety of diving-related careers. Your journey doesn’t end with certification; it’s just the beginning of a thrilling professional path.
### Navigating the PADI Zero to Hero Journey:
Embarking on the Odyssey: Your expedition commences with the foundational PADI Open Water Diver course. Here, you establish core skills, learn essential safety measures, and immerse yourself in the wonders of underwater exploration.
Ascending to Proficiency: The PADI Advanced Open Water Diver course takes you deeper into the world of diving. Gain expertise in specialized diving areas while relishing the thrill of underwater adventures.
Mastering Rescue Techniques: Elevate your diving prowess with the PADI Rescue Diver course. Learn to anticipate and manage potential diving emergencies, boosting your confidence as a responsible diver.
Becoming a Divemaster: As a PADI Divemaster candidate, you’ll be prepared to take on leadership roles. Assisting instructors and guiding fellow divers, you’ll shape the diving experiences of others
