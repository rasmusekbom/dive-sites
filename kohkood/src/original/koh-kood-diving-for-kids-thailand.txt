## How Koh Kood Diving for Kids Works (Age-Based Programs)
At Koh Kood Divers, we offer two different kids diving programs, depending on age. Therefore, every child enjoys a safe and fun experience that matches their comfort level and abilities.
### For Kids Aged 8–9: Bubble Maker Program
First, children aged 8–9 join the PADI Bubble Maker program in Koh Kood. This program is designed especially for young kids who want to try breathing underwater in a safe and playful way.
Then, with a PADI instructor, kids make shallow dives in calm water (maximum 2 meters). In addition, all equipment is child-sized, and the instructor stays very close at all times. As a result, kids feel safe, relaxed, and excited about their first underwater experience.
### For Kids Aged 10+: Discover Scuba Diving in Koh Kood
Next, children aged 10 and above can join Discover Scuba Diving in Koh Kood, which is a real scuba diving experience. First, they receive a simple safety briefing and equipment explanation. Then, they enter the water directly from the boat at a calm reef site around Koh Kood.
Moreover, kids complete one or two shallow dives with a professional PADI instructor. Because the reefs are shallow and conditions are calm, Discover Scuba Diving in Koh Kood is perfect for first-time young divers.
### Small Groups & Personal Attention
In addition, all kids diving programs in Koh Kood are conducted in very small groups. Therefore, each child receives personal attention, extra safety, and plenty of time to enjoy the dive without pressure.
### Boat-Based Program with Breaks
After each dive, kids return to the boat for rest, snacks, and drinks. Meanwhile, parents can relax and watch from the boat or join them in the water. Finally, the boat returns to Koh Kood with happy kids and unforgettable family memories.
### Why Parents Choose Koh Kood Diving for Kids
Because Koh Kood is quiet, uncrowded, and calm, it is ideal for kids diving. Moreover, all programs are run by experienced PADI instructors who specialize in working with children. As a result, parents feel confident while kids have fun exploring the underwater world of Koh Kood.
### Safety Standards for Kids Diving in Koh Kood
All kids diving programs follow official PADI standards for children, using child-sized equipment and slow, gentle teaching methods. Therefore, safety always comes first when diving with kids in Koh Kood.
### Continue Your Adventure with the PADI Open Water Diver Course in Koh Kood
If your child or teenager loves their first dive experience, the next step is the PADI Open Water Diver course in Koh Kood, which allows them to become fully certified divers from the age of 10. This course is perfect for families who want to continue diving together during their holiday.
#### What is PADI Discover scuba?
PADI Discover Scuba is an introductory diving experience designed for beginners who want to try scuba diving without committing to a full certification course. It allows participants to dive under the direct supervision of a certified PADI professional. During the experience, you learn basic safety rules and essential skills before exploring the underwater world in a controlled environment.
#### How deep is the PADI Discover scuba dive?
The PADI Discover Scuba dive allows participants to reach a maximum depth of 12 meters. However, the exact depth depends on comfort level and instructor supervision. Because this program is designed for beginners, instructors carefully monitor each dive to ensure safety and confidence underwater.
#### How long does Discover scuba take?
Discover Scuba typically takes half a day to complete. The program includes a short theory briefing, skill practice in shallow water, and at least one open water dive. Therefore, it is ideal for travelers who want to experience scuba diving without spending several days in training.
#### Can a Divemaster teach Discover scuba?
No, only a certified PADI Instructor can independently conduct the PADI Discover Scuba program. However, a Divemaster may assist under the direct supervision of an instructor. This ensures that all safety standards are maintained throughout the experience.
#### What is a Discover scuba course?
A Discover Scuba course is a beginner-friendly diving experience that introduces participants to basic scuba skills and underwater breathing techniques. Unlike full certification courses, it does not result in a permanent diving license. Instead, it provides a safe and supervised introduction to scuba diving.
