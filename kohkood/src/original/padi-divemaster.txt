### What is a PADI Divemaster?
A PADI Divemaster is the first professional level in scuba diving. It allows you to supervise certified divers and assist instructors during training.
At Koh Kood Divers, the program combines theory and practical dive experience to prepare you for a professional diving career.
### How long does it take to become a PADI Divemaster?
The PADI Divemaster course at Koh Kood Divers takes 4–8 weeks, depending on your availability and commitment. A 4-week intensive option is also available for divers with limited time, while the flexible structure allows you to progress at your own pace.
### How many dives do you need for a Divemaster?
To start the PADI Divemaster course, you need at least 40 logged dives plus Open Water, Advanced Open Water, and Rescue Diver certifications.
During the program at Koh Kood Divers, you complete between 40 and 100 dives around Koh Kood, Koh Rang, and the nearby wreck, gaining strong real-world experience.
### What does it take to become a PADI Divemaster?
To enroll, you must be at least 18 years old, in good health, hold Open Water, Advanced Open Water, and Rescue Diver certifications, and have 40 logged dives.
The course includes theory, water skills, stamina tests, and practical leadership training.

Divemaster salaries in Thailand vary by location and season, but typically start around 30,000 THB per month and can increase with experience and workload.
