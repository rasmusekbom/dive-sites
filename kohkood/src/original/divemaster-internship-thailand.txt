## Divemaster Internship Thailand — Train Beyond the Standards
Our Divemaster Internship Goes Further
Where most dive schools stop at assisting courses and leading dives, we focus heavily on teaching skills. In our Divemaster Internship Thailand, you will learn to recognise and correct the mistakes beginner divers make — not just manage them. Our programme includes an integrated workshop on the psychology of teaching diving, based on The Dive Professional’s Guide to Practical Psychology by Marc Luxen. We also include the PADI Discover Scuba Diving Leader Internship in every programme, certifying you to conduct the skills portion of DSD programmes — something most Divemaster graduates simply cannot do. We understand you want to make a few dives before starting your Dive Master Internship. That is why the program starts with a few days of just doing fun dives, so you are comfortable with the underwater environment around Koh Kood.
### Whether you are beginning with your Open Water certification or joining as a Rescue Diver — the program adapts to your current experience and builds from there.
Over four to eight weeks, this Divemaster Internship Thailand trains you as a PADI Divemaster in our working dive centre.You dive regularly, learn the craft properly, and gain the experience that comes from doing the job day after day. You will have a mentor with you throughout, a SIM card with unlimited internet, and everything arranged from the moment you land in Bangkok.
### More than a Divemaster Course in Thailand
Most Divemaster courses focus on guiding certified divers and assisting instructors. Our program goes further. With us, you will also learn the teaching side of diving — how to brief skills clearly, observe students underwater, and evaluate their performance. These are foundations of instructor training, and they are not part of a normal Divemaster course.
In a typical Divemaster training, you might learn how to demonstrate a skill after the instructor has already taught the student. In our program, you learn how to teach the skill yourself. You brief the skill, observe the student carefully underwater, and evaluate whether they performed it correctly and — more importantly — safely. This is a key difference in our Divemaster Internship Thailand
We also go deeper into the human side of diving: understanding how people think and respond underwater, how to read stress, how to manage a group with different personalities and backgrounds, and how to lead with confidence. Theory is taught in person — not just via e-learning. Understanding this is essential in any professional Divemaster Internship Thailand.
The result: you finish with greater knowledge, stronger skills, and far more confidence than a typical Divemaster candidate. And if you are planning a career in diving, you will arrive at your Instructor Course ahead of the pack.
Training is organised around half-days, leaving the rest of the day free for your own activities — working remotely, exploring the island, or joining one of our optional Muay Thai or yoga packages.
