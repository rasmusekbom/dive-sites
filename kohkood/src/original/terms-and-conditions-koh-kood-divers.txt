# Terms and Conditions – Koh Kood Divers
Equipment ResponsibilityThe client is responsible for the safekeeping of all equipment issued for use during any activity or course.
With the exception of fair wear and tear, Koh Kood Divers reserves the right to charge for equipment that has been lost, damaged, or misused.
Cancellations and RefundsWe arrange food, taxis, and boat staff the day before your planned trip. If you wish to cancel, please notify us before 5:00 PM the day prior to your trip to receive a refund of your deposit.
Cancellations made after this deadline will result in no refund of the deposit.
Taxi ServiceThe taxi arranged by Koh Kood Divers will wait a maximum of 10 minutes at the agreed pickup location.
If the client fails to show up within this time, it will be considered a cancellation, and no refund will be possible.
The client is responsible for providing an accurate pickup location.
Study Materials and CoursesFor PADI and other diving courses, once digital or physical study materials have been assigned to the client, they are non-refundable.
PaymentFull payment must be made before the start of the activity or course.
Medical & Fitness to DiveClients must complete the standard PADI Medical Questionnaire (or equivalent).
If any medical conditions are indicated, a doctor’s clearance is required before participating.
Koh Kood Divers reserves the right to refuse service if a client is deemed unfit to dive, for safety reasons.
Certification RequirementsCertified divers must present a valid certification card (physical or digital) before participating in fun dives.
Diving beyond your certification level is not permitted.
Environmental ResponsibilityTouching or collecting marine life, standing on coral, or using non-reef-safe sunscreen is strictly prohibited.
Clients are expected to follow all environmental guidelines explained during the briefing.
Alcohol & Substance UseParticipation is not allowed under the influence of alcohol or drugs. If a client arrives intoxicated, the activity will be cancelled with no refund.
Children & Minors
Minimum ages apply for courses and activities (per PADI standards).
A parent or legal guardian must sign consent forms for participants under 18 years of age.
Food Allergies
The client is responsible for informing Koh Kood Divers of any food allergies or dietary restrictions.
This information must be provided at the time of booking and confirmed again when food is served.
Koh Kood Divers cannot be held responsible for allergic reactions if this information is not communicated properly by the client.
Personal Belongings
Koh Kood Divers is not responsible for lost or damaged personal items during activities or while on the dive boat.
