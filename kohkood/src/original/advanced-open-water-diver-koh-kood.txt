## PADI Advanced Open Water Diver : Dive into adventure!
Experience the thrill of the underwater world with the PADI Advanced Open Water Course . In just 2 or 3 days, you can complete 5 exciting adventure dives. Two dives, deep dive and navigation, are mandatory, while you get to choose 3 more from 15 incredible options like wreck, drift, and night diving.
### Choosing your advanced dives:
 Meet your instructor and discuss your preferences to select your 3 additional adventure dives. Complete relevant chapters in the Adventures in Diving manual and knowledge reviews. We can provide a PADI e-book for pre-course reading at home.
 Compared to Open Water, this course is fun-focused with minimal theory. After completion, you’ll be certified to dive up to 30 meters deep, opening doors to new underwater possibilities.
### PADI ADVANCED OPEN WATER DIVER DAY 1:
 We’ll pick you up between 8:15 and 8:50 AM, taking you to Bang Bao bay. At our dive center, you’ll have a dive briefing and discuss the day’s plan with your instructor. Arriving at the dive site around 10:00 AM, you’ll complete 2 dives with lunch in-between. Afterward, review knowledge with your instructor and debrief the dives. Return to Koh Kood by 2:15-3:00 PM.
### PADI ADVANCED OPEN WATER DIVER DAY 2:
 Get ready for another thrilling day! The schedule is similar, with an additional 3rd dive, possibly a night dive. We’ll pick you up from your resort around 5:30 PM, returning around 7:30 PM. After completing your dives, return to the dive school to finish knowledge reviews and process your PADI Advanced Diver certification.
### Loved the PADI Advanced Diver course?
Continue your diving adventure by enrolling in the PADI Rescue Course (2-3 days). Learn dive safety, accident prevention and response, to become a true hero.
