#### What is PADI Open Water?
PADI Open Water is the world’s most popular beginner scuba diving certification. It is the first level of professional dive training and allows you to dive independently with a buddy to a maximum depth of 18 meters. The course combines theory lessons, confined water practice, and four open water dives. After successful completion, you receive a lifetime certification that is recognized worldwide.
#### How many dives do you need for PADI Open Water?
To earn your PADI Open Water certification, you must complete four open water dives. In addition, students practice essential scuba skills in confined water before entering the ocean. These four dives allow you to apply what you learned during training while gaining real underwater experience in a safe and controlled environment.
#### How far do you have to swim for PADI Open Water?
For the PADI Open Water course, you need to swim 200 meters without stopping. However, there is no time limit, and you may use any swimming style. Alternatively, you can complete a 300-meter swim using mask, snorkel, and fins. You must also float or tread water for 10 minutes to demonstrate basic comfort in the water.
