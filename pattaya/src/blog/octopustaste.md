Octopus scientific name is – Octopus vulgaris. They are invertebrates which are found in all oceans, however, seem to be most abundant in tropical water.

They are special invertebrates – them and a common vertebrate would seem to have nothing in common. Yet, due to an octopus’ cognitive complexity, they seem to have both short and long term memory alongside with the ability to recognize people and explore objects with all 8 long arms they have.

These 8 long limbs contain an abundance of neurons. Allowing these octopus to feel and taste independently without having signals come from the brain.
