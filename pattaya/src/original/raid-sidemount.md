## Frequent Questions
#### Can I get my PADI with you?
At Thai Ocean Academy we focus on training the RAID way. We find that RAID is better aligned with our pace and high standards of diver training. The certifications issued are recognized world wide, and are accepted by all other major agencies
#### What Is the benefit of Sidemount?
The benefits of Sidemount are many. Firstly you have 2 redundant air sources, this means you have double the air and systems to help your self if there is ever a failure. It also provides a more streamlined way through the water, and allows you to look super super cool!
