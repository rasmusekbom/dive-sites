## Frequent Questions
#### I Cannot swim, Can I dive?
For basic diving experiences, no experience is necessary. Our instructors are patient and will take the time to make sure you are comfortable and safe before entering the water!
#### Will I get Photos?
Our team will always strive to take photos and videos of you underwater, however due to laws and regulations in Thailand, instructors supervising activities such as try dive cannot carry cameras.
At Thai Ocean Academy we have dedicated photographers for photos and videos daily, that will always try and get you free photos of your experience!
