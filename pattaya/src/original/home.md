# Diving Pattaya
Educate, Equip, Explore
Scuba Diving in Pattaya is always interesting. We have shipwrecks, coral sites, and restoration sites! For first time try divers or experienced technical divers we will get you on the site that suits you. Our team is ready to help you have an awesome experience diving with us!
