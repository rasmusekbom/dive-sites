## Frequent Questions
#### Can I get my PADI with you?
At Thai Ocean Academy we focus on training the RAID way. We find that RAID is better aligned with our pace and high standards of diver training. The certifications issued are recognized world wide, and are accepted by all other major agencies
#### Will I have to re-do my entire Instructor Development Course (IDC) or take an IE?
No. Because you are already a certified instructor in good standing, you do not need to repeat basic instructional methodology or take another formal Instructor Examination (IE). The RAID Crossover focuses specifically on orienting you to RAID standards, mastering our digital learning portal, and demonstrating RAID’s signature high-performance, neutrally-buoyant teaching techniques.
