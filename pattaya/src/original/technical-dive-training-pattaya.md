## Technical Dive training
Our technical diver training programs are supported by an experienced, and fun community of local tech divers. Our Technical instructors, led by Douglas Docherty of [Dark Water Diving Asia](https://darkwaterdiving.asia/), offer unparalleled experience, advice and patience for your technical diving journey. We firmly believe in quality over quantity, and dedicate the time and space needed for you to have the best experience possible
To begin your journey into technical diving, most people will start with a basic Nitrox/Decompression course, providing you with the fundamental knowledge required. These, combined along with sidemount or twinset training, will open the doors and depths to everything else you dream of diving. Below is a list of our most common courses.
- [Sidemount Training](https://www.pattaya-dive.com/raid-sidemount/)
- [RAID Nitrox Plus (40m)](https://www.pattaya-dive.com/raid-nitrox-plus/)
- [TDI ANDP 45m (Advanced Nitrox Decompression Procedures)](https://www.pattaya-dive.com/tdi-andp/)
- [RAID Decompression Diver(45m)](https://www.pattaya-dive.com/raid-full-decompression-diver/)
- [RAID Advanced Decompression Diver(50m)](https://www.pattaya-dive.com/raid-extended-decompression-diver/)
- [RAID Normoxic Decompression Diver (60m)](https://www.pattaya-dive.com/technical-wreck-diver-training/)
