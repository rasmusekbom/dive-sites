# Scuba Dive locations
## Thai Ocean Academy locations
Thai Ocean Academy offers a range of locations to cater to your diving needs. In Bangkok, you can find our well-stocked equipment store, providing everything you need for your underwater adventures. Pattaya is a popular destination for scuba diving, with its vibrant marine life and diverse dive sites. Koh Chang is another fantastic location for scuba diving, offering pristine waters and stunning coral reefs and the HTMS Chang. Additionally, we are excited to announce that Samaesan will be joining our lineup of diving destinations in the near future.
