Explore Top Scuba Diving Spots in Thailand: Rayong

Thailand’s coastal beauty extends beyond Pattaya, offering incredible scuba diving experiences in Rayong. These destinations are perfect for divers seeking vibrant marine life, crystal-clear waters, and diverse underwater landscapes. Discover why Rayong should be on your list for your next diving adventure.

Rayong: A Hidden Diving Gem

Rich Marine Ecosystem

Rayong’s dive sites are teeming with marine life. From colorful coral reefs to diverse fish species, you’ll encounter a vibrant underwater world. Dive into areas surrounded by hard and soft corals, schools of yellowfin fish, spotted stingrays, sea turtles, and various species of sea slugs. The variety of marine life makes every dive an adventure.

Top Dive Sites in Rayong

Hin Farang: This site features a small pile of rocks between Koh Mun Nai and Koh Mun Klang. Ideal for beginners, it offers a depth range of 5-13 meters and is surrounded by rough sand. Dive into clear waters and discover hard and soft corals, yellowfin fish, spotted stingrays, and sea turtles.

Hin Ploeng East: Known for its vibrant orange-red anemones, this site provides a stunning underwater view. With a maximum depth of 25 meters, divers can explore a large underwater rock pile covered in anemones, soft corals, and various sea slugs. The site is popular for underwater photography.

HTMS Petra: A former naval ship turned artificial reef, HTMS Petra offers an exciting wreck diving experience. Sunk in 2012, this site is now a thriving habitat for marine life, including barracudas, sea slugs, and various corals. Dive to a maximum depth of 25 meters and explore the ship’s structure.

Why Choose Rayong?

Rayong offers a serene diving experience away from the crowds, making it perfect for those seeking tranquility and rich marine biodiversity. Its diverse dive sites cater to all levels, ensuring a memorable adventure for every diver.

Experience the best of Thailand’s scuba diving by venturing beyond Pattaya to the beautiful and diverse dive sites of Rayong. Your underwater adventure awaits!
